LOGITRACK SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-SHIP-001

1. DELIVERY TIMEFRAMES BY REGION

LogiTrack aims to deliver all orders within the following standard windows,
measured from the date of shipment confirmation:

- East Region: 3-6 business days (standard), 1-2 business days (express)
- West Region: 4-7 business days (standard), 2-3 business days (express)
- North Region: 3-5 business days (standard), 1-2 business days (express)
- South Region: 4-6 business days (standard), 2-3 business days (express)

Delivery estimates exclude weekends and public holidays. Rural or remote
addresses within any region may experience an additional 1-3 business days.

2. CARRIER PARTNERS

LogiTrack partners with FedEx, UPS, DHL, and USPS. Carrier assignment is
determined automatically based on package weight, destination, and current
carrier capacity. Customers may not request a specific carrier for standard
shipping tiers.

3. DELAYED SHIPMENTS

A shipment is classified as "Delayed" if it has not arrived within the
standard delivery window stated in Section 1. When a shipment is marked
Delayed in our tracking system:

- The customer is automatically notified via email within 24 hours of the
  delay being detected.
- Customers may request a delivery status update at any time by contacting
  support with their order ID.
- If a shipment remains undelivered 5 business days beyond the original
  estimate, the customer becomes eligible for a partial shipping cost refund
  as described in the Refund Policy (POL-REF-001), Section 3.

4. LOST SHIPMENTS

A shipment is classified as "Lost" if there has been no tracking update for
10 consecutive business days. Lost shipments are eligible for full refund
or replacement at the customer's choice, per Refund Policy Section 4.

5. VEHICLE AND FLEET OPERATIONS

Regional deliveries within East, West, North, and South are supported by
LogiTrack's owned fleet vehicles for last-mile delivery in major
metropolitan areas, supplementing third-party carrier networks. Fleet
vehicle assignments and routes are managed through the internal logistics
system and are not customer-facing.

LOGITRACK REFUND & RETURN POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-REF-001

1. GENERAL RETURN WINDOW

Most items purchased through LogiTrack-fulfilled orders may be returned
within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item is unused
and in its original packaging. Certain product categories (see Section 5)
have modified return windows.

2. HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN

Customers should contact support with their order ID and reason for return.
Once approved, a prepaid return shipping label is issued. Refunds are
processed within 5-7 business days of the returned item being received at
our fulfillment center.

3. DELAYED SHIPMENT REFUNDS

If an order is marked "Delayed" per Shipping Policy Section 3, and remains
undelivered 5 business days beyond the original delivery estimate, the
customer is automatically eligible for a refund of 50% of the shipping cost
paid on that order. This refund does not require the customer to initiate a
return and is credited automatically to the original payment method within
3 business days of eligibility being confirmed.

4. LOST SHIPMENT REFUNDS

If a shipment is classified as "Lost" per Shipping Policy Section 4, the
customer may choose between:
  (a) A full refund of the order total, including original shipping cost, or
  (b) A full replacement shipment at no additional cost.

5. NON-RETURNABLE AND MODIFIED-WINDOW ITEMS

- Office furniture (e.g., desks, chairs): 14-day return window due to
  assembly/wear considerations, subject to a 15% restocking fee if assembled.
- Electronics (e.g., headphones, webcams): standard 30-day window, but must
  include all original accessories and packaging.
- Custom or made-to-order items: non-returnable except in cases of defect.

6. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS

Items arriving damaged or defective are eligible for full refund or free
replacement regardless of the standard return window, provided the customer
reports the issue within 7 days of delivery with photo documentation.

7. CANCELLED ORDERS

Orders may be cancelled free of charge any time before the "Shipped" status
is reached. Once a shipment has been dispatched, standard return procedures
in Sections 1-2 apply instead of cancellation.
